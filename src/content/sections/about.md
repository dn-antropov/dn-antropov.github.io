I am a creative technologist with an unusual background. Despite earning a PhD in art history,
I have always been fascinated by computers and by the way they let us create complex self-organizing systems, 
simulations and games. I learned to program in Delphi in the 5th grade, but only took programming seriously 
when decided to pursue a career as a media artist.

My developer's journey began with making graphics and interactive installations for exhibitions and museums 
in live-programming environment [vvvv](https://vvvv.org/). Eventually, I started working for interactive design agencies as a developer
of real-time AR and VR experiences. When the industry shifted to the Unity and Unreal engines, I pivoted to working with them as well.

I gradually taught myself everything I had missed by not having a computer science degree. My motto is "learning by doing".
Finishing my PhD taught me how to research complicated topics and solve hard problems with enough dedication.
