title: Personal
---

Coming from a media art background I recently took making games seriously. I see games as an ultimate form of expression
and as something that I always wanted to do but was ap of complexity.
As much I feel at home inside of Unreal Engine and use it daily at work I try to
break loose from "Epic" way of doing things. I make my personal games either inside of Godot or plain C/C++ code. Here
you can find some of the games that I released or am actively working on.

Another passion of mine is mathematical beauty of shaders. I always try to incorporate some snippet of shader code into
everything I do. You saw example of this on top of the page inside the parody game about squeezing the Penrose triangle
or in any of my games on that page.

I am not a hater of artificial intelligence. I was always genuinely curious about the application of deep learning in the fields of
computer vision, speech, text and object recognition. I used it widely in my daily work and experimented a lot with it
in my spare time. I was always a big proponent of automatization of routine tasks nobody cared about or wanted to do.

When it comes to using generative AI for human expression be it art or coding I am not a believer or supporter.
I value human craft, learning by overcoming challenges and human care. I see how people in the industry
that I am working right now outsource their craft and thinking to generative AI and don't want to follow this path.
That's why I found my happy place in the games that I am making. There I do not use generative AI at all and
make all the things by hand. I enjoy the process of writing code as much of the process of drawing or making music as
soundtracks, and I don't let AI take it away from me.
