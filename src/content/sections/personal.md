title: Personal
---

Coming from a media art background, I have recently started taking game development seriously.
Games are the ultimate form of expression and I find the most joy in making them.

For my personal indie projects I rarely use Unreal Engine. It's too bloated when it comes to small stuff. For my small games
I use Godot or plain C/C++ that allow me to do things quickly and efficiently.

Another passion of mine is the mathematical beauty of shaders. It is easier to write a shader
than to keyframe an animation. You’ll find examples of these procedural approaches in all of my projects.

I have experimented with GPT-2 and Stable Diffusion some years ago, when those things were mostly broken, quirky and funny.
Nowadays, generative AI has become "too perfect" and boring for me to find any inspiration in it.
